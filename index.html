What I do

I'm a Senior Software Engineer on the Gallup AI team, working on the shared foundation the Gallup AI products are built on: retrieval, agents, evaluation, and the production systems around them on AWS, from RFC to running in production.

One of those systems is described in an AWS Architecture Blog post I co-authored: Gallup delivers real-time workplace coaching to thousands of leaders with Amazon Bedrock.

How I work

I learn by building. When I need to understand something properly, I work through it with runnable code, write it up, and check the claims against primary sources. Most of what I publish is that habit made visible.

The layers I keep coming back to are the ones an AI product actually stands on: the data underneath it, the identity and access around it, the retrieval that feeds it, and the evaluation that tells you whether any of it works.
